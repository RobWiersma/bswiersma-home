LovelyOSD

Experimental · personal project

A browser-based FPV video editor that synchronizes Betaflight blackbox logs with flight footage. It recreates the on-screen display in the goggles' original font, grades video, mixes audio, and renders finished videos or YouTube Shorts directly on the GPU. It also manages quadcopters and tune history, with a beta Inspector for detecting objects in inspection footage.
